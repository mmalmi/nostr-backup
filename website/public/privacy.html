Social Backup

Privacy policy

Effective November 15, 2026 · Chrome and Firefox extensions

Your archive and key

Social Backup stores your account settings, configured usernames, captured posts, local drafts, signed Nostr events, attachments, delivery records and website signing approvals in your browser profile. Creating a profile publishes the name, description, picture, banner and website you choose, with your public key, to your selected Nostr relays; its pictures are copied to the media backup server selected in Media so they stay available. Signing in with a public key lets you import that account’s events read-only. The secret key, if supplied or generated, stays in the extension’s local database, unencrypted at rest while unlocked. Someone with access to your browser profile files could recover it.

Signing up

To fill in sign-up, the extension reads which X and Instagram accounts are signed in on tabs you already have open, and on Instagram that account’s public name, description, link and picture. This stays on the device. If you enter an X username, it loads that public X profile without your cookies to suggest your name, picture, banner, description and website. When you sign in with an existing key, it reads your published identity list from your relays and checks its X proof on X.

Backing up and publishing

When you configure a social account, the extension reads matching posts as you browse supported profiles. It checks public originals with X, Facebook or Instagram without account cookies. It can send confirmed public posts, their authors, original URLs, timestamps and media references to the Nostr relays selected in Backup, and public attachments or index blocks to the media backup servers selected in Media. Capture may publish automatically. Private and unconfirmed captures stay on the device and can be included in your ZIP export. A flower with a check marks your own posts on X, Facebook and Instagram once they are in your backup. For this the extension compares the IDs of posts on the page with your backup on the device and sends nothing; you can turn the marks off in Sources. A click on the mark of a post that has a public copy opens that copy on iris.to in a new tab. The extension opens the tab, so the page you were on does not get the address of the copy. On these sites the toolbar icon also shows a dot: green while your backup is on, yellow when it needs you and red when posts on the page are not saved. The extension works this out on the device, from your settings and the same comparison of post IDs, and sends nothing. Relay sync requests signed events for your public key, which can include encrypted messages.

Media downloads contact the original file host or a configured server. On other HTTPS pages the extension can resolve supported media links using the local archive, configured media backup servers and, for named trees, Nostr relays. Those services receive the requested media hash or tree identifier. Remote services can see your IP address and the time of a request. Public posts and shared archive links can be read, copied and retained by others.

Account links, tips and signing

Posting an X verification can publish an association between your X handle and Nostr key once the post shows on any X page, or when you paste its link; the extension confirms it on X’s public page first. The extension stores handles from your configured X following list locally. Finding matches checks claimed proof posts with X. Following a match publishes your updated Nostr contact list.

The extension refreshes a directory of public Nostr identity claims from your selected relays in the background. Matching X handles happens on the device. When the toolbar popup opens on an X profile that has a claim in this directory, it checks the X proof on X and shows that Nostr profile from the directory, with its known followers from a snapshot included in the extension. Relays are not asked about the profiles you view.

Bitcoin tips are optional. Opening a tip checks the public X proof and queries the Nostr author, profile and, where applicable, the matching note and tip receipts. Currency estimates use public rate events or Coinbase. Selecting an amount requests an invoice from the recipient’s Lightning provider. The provider receives the amount and may receive a signed zap request naming your public key, the recipient, relays and the Nostr note. A provider can publish a receipt. Your wallet authorizes payment. Turning off tips removes the tip buttons.

When a website requests Nostr sign-in or a signature, approval lets it receive your public key or the signed event. You can remember allowing or declining a kind of request for a site and revoke this in Account. The extension does not give the site your secret key.

Retention and choices

You can disable capture per platform, change relay and media destinations, download a ZIP, lock the key, revoke website permissions or delete the local archive in Account. Exported files remain wherever you saved them. Deleting local data cannot withdraw records already delivered to other services.

Social Backup has no advertising or analytics service and does not sell user data. It uses data for the features described here. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

For questions about the extension, use the project support page. Keep keys, private posts and archive files out of public issues. See also the terms of use.
