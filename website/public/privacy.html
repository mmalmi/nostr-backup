Social Backup

Privacy policy

Effective November 14, 2026 · Chrome and Firefox extensions

Your archive and key

Social Backup stores your account settings, configured usernames, captured posts, local drafts, signed Nostr events, attachments, delivery records and website signing approvals in your browser profile. Creating a profile publishes your chosen name and public key to your selected Nostr relays. Signing in with a public key lets you import that account’s events read-only. The secret key, if supplied or generated, stays in the extension’s local database, unencrypted at rest while unlocked. Someone with access to your browser profile files could recover it.

Backing up and publishing

When you configure a social account, the extension reads matching posts as you browse supported profiles. It checks public originals with X, Facebook or Instagram without account cookies. It can send confirmed public posts, their authors, original URLs, timestamps and media references to the Nostr relays selected in Backup, and public attachments or index blocks to the Blossom servers selected in Media. Capture may publish automatically. Private and unconfirmed captures stay on the device and can be included in your ZIP export. Relay sync requests signed events for your public key, which can include encrypted messages.

Media downloads contact the original file host or a configured server. On other HTTPS pages the extension can resolve supported media links using the local archive, configured Blossom servers and, for named trees, Nostr relays. Those services receive the requested media hash or tree identifier. Remote services can see your IP address and the time of a request. Public posts and shared archive links can be read, copied and retained by others.

Account links, tips and signing

Posting an X verification and backing it up can publish an association between your X handle and Nostr key. The extension stores handles from your configured X following list locally. Finding matches checks claimed proof posts with X. Following a match publishes your updated Nostr contact list.

Bitcoin tips are optional. Enabling tips refreshes a directory of public Nostr claims from your selected relays. Matching X handles while scrolling happens on the device. Opening a tip checks the public X proof and queries the Nostr author, profile and, where applicable, the matching note and tip receipts. Currency estimates use public rate events or Coinbase. Selecting an amount requests an invoice from the recipient’s Lightning provider. The provider receives the amount and may receive a signed zap request naming your public key, the recipient, relays and the Nostr note. A provider can publish a receipt. Your wallet authorizes payment. Turning off tips stops tip buttons and scheduled tip-directory updates.

When a website requests Nostr sign-in or a signature, approval lets it receive your public key or the signed event. You can remember permissions for a site and revoke them in Account. The extension does not give the site your secret key.

Retention and choices

You can disable capture per platform, change relay and media destinations, download a ZIP, lock the key, revoke website permissions or delete the local archive in Account. Exported files remain wherever you saved them. Deleting local data cannot withdraw records already delivered to other services.

Social Backup has no advertising or analytics service and does not sell user data. It uses data for the features described here. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

For questions about the extension, use the project support page. Keep keys, private posts and archive files out of public issues.
